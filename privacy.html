SkyPicks Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

SkyPicks is a free app, made by one person, that helps you pick what to watch from an airline's inflight catalog. This policy explains exactly what the app does and doesn't do with your information. The short version: you can use the whole app without an account, and if you do that, nothing about you leaves your phone.

Using SkyPicks without an account

No sign-in is required. Your watchlist, the titles you've marked seen, your ratings, and your settings are stored only on your device. I cannot see them, and there is no account associated with you.

The app does make ordinary network requests to fetch content. Like any web request, these reveal your IP address and approximate region to the servers involved, which use it to deliver the response:

RequestGoes toWhat is sent
Airline catalogs and the airline list GitHub Pages (funkmesh.github.io) Which catalog file you're loading. Nothing about you.
Poster images The airline's media CDN (cms-scdn.airtime.geemedia.com) Which poster is being displayed.
"Search any title" (only when you use it) Wikidata (www.wikidata.org), operated by the Wikimedia Foundation The words you typed in that search box.

These requests carry no account identifier, no advertising identifier, and no device fingerprint.

Signing in (optional)

If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google — from the one-time offer at first launch, or from Settings — SkyPicks backs your lists up so they follow you to your other devices. Sign-in is entirely optional and you can decline it forever without losing any feature except cross-device sync.

When you are signed in, the following is stored in Google's Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on servers in the United States:

  • Your email address and a random account identifier, from your Apple or Google account. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", I only ever receive Apple's relay address — not your real one.
  • Your lists — the titles you've marked seen, rated, watchlisted, or marked not-interested, with the title, year, type, and the time you last changed each one.

That data is used for one purpose: storing your lists and syncing them to your devices. It is not used for advertising or profiling. Google acts as my processor for storage and sign-in; see the Firebase privacy documentation. I do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

What SkyPicks never does

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage-tracking SDKs. I do not know which screens you open or which titles you look at.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites, in the App Store's sense of the word.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Diagnostics and feedback

The app keeps a local error log on your device so that a crash can be explained later. It is never uploaded automatically. If you tap Send feedback with app data in Settings, the app builds a ZIP of your app data (your lists, cached catalogs, settings, and that error log) and hands it to your mail app so you can review it before sending it to developerloopy@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. Emails you send me are kept only as long as needed to deal with the issue.

Deleting your data

Without an account: Settings › Clear watch history erases your lists, or just delete the app.

With an account: Settings › Account › Delete account permanently deletes your stored lists and the account itself, on every device. Where you signed in with Apple, the app also revokes SkyPicks' access token with Apple. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Your lists are kept for as long as your account exists — there is no other retention period, because there is nothing else stored. If you'd rather I delete something by hand, email developerloopy@gmail.com.

Children

SkyPicks is not directed at children and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has a "kids titles" filter, but it is a way to filter a movie catalog, not a service for children.

Your rights

Because the only personal data I hold is an email address and your own lists, you can exercise the usual rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) directly in the app: your lists are visible in My Lists, and Delete account removes everything. For anything else, email me.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, I'll update this page and the date at the top, and note it in the App Store release notes.
