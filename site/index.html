SkyPicks

Know what's worth watching before you board.

Airline entertainment catalogs are a wall of a thousand titles with no ratings and no order. SkyPicks pulls the catalog for your airline, ranks it by critic and audience scores plus awards, learns what you like, and remembers what you've already seen — so you can pick a film in a minute instead of scrolling through the seat-back screen after takeoff.

  • Best Picks — the catalog ranked best-first, filtered to what fits your flight length.
  • Works offline — load it before you fly; everything but online search keeps working in the air.
  • Your lists — watchlist, seen, and ratings that carry across airlines, with optional sync across your devices.
  • United, American, and Delta, switchable in a tap.

Free, no ads, no tracking. iPhone and iPad, iOS 15+.
