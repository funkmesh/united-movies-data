SkyPicks Support

Help, answers, and how to reach a human.

Fastest way to get help: in the app, go to Settings › Send feedback with app data. That drafts an email with a copy of your app state attached, which usually tells me exactly what went wrong. You can read it before sending.

Or just email developerloopy@gmail.com. It's one person, so give me a day or two.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works signed out, and your lists stay on your device. Signing in with Apple or Google only adds a backup that syncs to your other devices.

Does it work in the air, in airplane mode?

Yes — that's the point. Load the catalog before you fly (open the app once on the ground) and everything works offline: Best Picks, Browse, your lists, and marking things seen. Only "search any title" and refreshing a catalog need a connection. Changes you make offline sync up next time you're online.

The catalog doesn't match what's on my flight

Airlines rotate catalogs monthly and vary them by aircraft and route, so there will be misses. Settings shows which month's catalog you have and lets you refresh it. If it's badly off, use Settings › Report a catalog problem — those reports are what keep the data honest.

Which airlines are supported?

United, American, and Delta today, switchable in Settings. Your lists carry across all of them. Want another airline? Email me — adding one is mostly a matter of finding a public catalog.

How are "Best Picks" chosen?

A blend of critical and audience scores (IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic), awards, and what you've liked or disliked — genres, directors, cast, and era. Rate a few titles and it gets noticeably better. Titles you've seen drop off by default; there's a toggle to keep them.

How do I delete my data?

Signed out: Settings › Clear watch history, or delete the app. Signed in: Settings › Account › Delete account removes your account and its stored lists everywhere, permanently. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this an official airline app?

No. SkyPicks is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any airline. It can't book flights, check you in, or play movies — it only helps you decide what to watch once you're on board.
